# Changelog

What changed in each TestFlight build of Lastide for iPhone, newest first. The app shows this file in Settings, What's New.

## Unreleased

- Settings has a What's New row. It lists the changes of every build, newest first, and marks the build on your phone.
- After an update, the app shows what is new in that build once.

## Build 8 (2026-11-24)

- Every number and bar on the Learning screen opens the list of cases behind it.
- Each case says why it counts: what it repeats, or when its group was first said.
- Open a case to see its details, such as its group, its rule, its draft or its drafting run.
- A message you typed offers Show My Words, which reads your words from your PC. The words leave the screen when you leave the app.
- A drafting run shows its Claude Code version, its models, its tokens and an estimated cost.
- In the Library, "Violations since approval" opens the rule's list of violations.

## Build 7 (2026-11-24)

- Fixed a crash when you tapped a notification.
- Fixed a crash when you used Approve or Delete on a notification.
- Approve and Delete still work only while your iPhone is unlocked, as in build 5.

## Build 6 (2026-11-23)

- Each paired PC shows in one row with its own Revoke button. Revoke still asks first.
- A green badge shows a PC that is online. A grey badge shows when an offline PC was last seen.
- The rows no longer repeat "Paired with", because the section title says it.
- Long PC names wrap, and the row stacks at large text sizes.

## Build 5 (2026-11-23)

- Turn on notifications in Settings to hear about new drafts. A notification shows the draft text and its scope.
- Approve, Delete or Open a draft from the notification. Approve and Delete work only while your iPhone is unlocked.
- A locked iPhone shows rule text only after you turn on "Show rule text while iPhone is locked".
- The widget shows how many rules wait for your review, on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.
- The app icon badge counts the rules that wait.
- A new pairing uses App Attest, so your PC can check that it talks to the real app.
- PCs you paired before this build stay paired.

## Build 4 (2026-11-23)

- The app now holds a notification extension. In this build it shows each notification as it arrives.
- The app now holds a Lastide widget. In this build it shows fixed text.
- These two parts get ready for notifications with actions, which arrive in build 5.

## Build 3 (2026-11-23)

- The Learning screen shows today's drafting runs and the daily limit on runs.
- The Learning screen shows how many imported principles wait for review on your PC.
- Evidence from a review comment shows the comment's label, such as "Review comment C7".
- A PC with an older Lastide keeps working with the phone.

## Build 2 (2026-11-20)

- Pair your iPhone with Lastide on your PC: scan the QR code on the PC, then check that both screens show the same code.
- Review the rule drafts your PC proposes. Approve, edit or delete each draft, and filter the queue.
- The Library lists the rules you approved, with their scope and their counts.
- The Learning screen shows repeat corrections and violations by week. A count your PC does not measure yet reads "Not measured yet".
- Settings lists your paired PCs and a Diagnostics block to copy into a support message.
- Without a paired PC, the app shows sample data.
